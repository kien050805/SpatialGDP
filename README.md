## Project Title
Application of NPP-VIIRS Nighttime Light Imagery for GDP Spatialization and Economic Differences in Vietnam

## Research Question
This project aims to investigate how NPP-VIIRS nighttime light imagery can be used to spatialize economic activity and identify regional economic differences in Vietnam. The main question is whether nighttime light intensity can serve as a useful proxy for GDP and provide a more detailed representation of economic activity across different regions. The project will use nighttime light imagery from the Earth Observation Group’s VIIRS nighttime light dataset, available from the Earth Observation Group, along with regional economic data, such as GDP by province or region. While GDP provides an important measure of economic activity, aggregated GDP statistics can obscure differences between regions and provide limited information about the geographic distribution of economic production. By analyzing the spatial distribution of nighttime lights and comparing it with regional GDP, this project will evaluate whether nighttime imagery can reveal patterns of economic activity that traditional GDP statistics may overlook. The results could also help identify geographic factors associated with regional economic differences, such as rivers, elevation, transportation networks, and urbanization. More broadly, this research could demonstrate how remotely sensed data can complement traditional economic statistics and provide researchers and policymakers with a more detailed understanding of regional economic development in Vietnam.
